NV2A Shader Translation Lab

Explore how original Xbox shader behavior can be represented as Vita-friendly Cg and GXM work.

← Project home

This interactive study is based on the public Halo CE Vita source architecture. It is an educational model of selected translation concepts—not a Direct3D 8 emulator, a complete NV2A implementation, or a compiler. The generated snippets are illustrative and must be validated against the original translator and Vita compiler.

5 · Source files to inspect

Accuracy and safety: The combiner playground models one RGB channel of the final-combiner equation only. Real NV2A pixel shaders include texture-stage dependencies, per-channel inputs, intermediate registers, mux modes, clamping between stages and separate alpha handling. The vertex explorer is a conceptual instruction mapping, not a binary NV2A disassembler. Do not include proprietary game assets, firmware modules, extracted data or console keys in this repository.
